# ReShiki local real-app check

Build under test: **main 1d94f392 (before)**, as supplied for this run. Tested on macOS on 2026-11-15 (Asia/Tokyo), using only the specified wrapper at `a ReShiki.app wrapper of the debug build` and the supplied `gallery.rsk`.

The requested workflow completed. The drawing, selection inspector, undo/redo, template thumbnails, and ring card all rendered and eventually responded. Operations involving the enlarged drawing had noticeable latency. Dark mode exposed a visual issue: the gallery's dark green headings and annotations had very low contrast on the black canvas.

## Friction

- **Copy/paste workflow:** Rapid Cmd+A, Cmd+C, Cmd+V produced “A clipboard operation is already in progress” instead of completing the paste. The first attempt left Undo disabled; later screenshots/count checks also showed that the second duplication had not yet occurred until paste was retried. This makes the prescribed keyboard sequence unreliable without waiting for copy completion and verifying the result.
- **Large-selection Properties:** Selecting the four-copy drawing took about 7 seconds to produce the inspector result. A canvas scroll while selected took about 5 seconds to return a settled view. These pauses slow inspection and navigation through the enlarged drawing.
- **History:** Redo of the last paste took about 16 seconds, substantially longer than the roughly 4-second undo. There was a long pause before the restored drawing could be checked.
- **Template browsing:** Thumbnails were complete in every observed view, but scrolling down and back took about 4 and 3 seconds respectively to return settled screenshots. The thumbnail names appeared in hover tooltips, and these tooltips covered part of the adjacent canvas while the pointer remained over a tile.
- **Ring-card access:** Clicking the main ring button selected the tool and exposed its top options; opening the illustrated Rings card required clicking the small corner triangle. The preview collection was not shown by the main button click alone.
- **Dark-mode canvas contrast:** Gallery headings and many text annotations stayed dark green on the black canvas, making them much harder to read even though bonds were light. The template tiles stayed white and remained readable. This is visible in `08-dark-templates.png`.
