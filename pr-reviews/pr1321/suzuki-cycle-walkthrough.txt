Suzuki–Miyaura coupling, circular insertion example

Captured in the macOS ReShiki app in Publication theme, with a light
canvas and 74% zoom throughout. The full window is 2560 by 1704.
Source commit: a11be2cf9e64148924cc582affb4f015c0f388d3
App SHA-256: 7b3740c802fd37138e6b707d4dca16d2a0d307c91da09ef4560d1d69eb5d074a

1. Open suzuki-cycle-before.rsk. The aryl–palladium bromide complex
   sits outside a cycle sketch with two placed intermediates.
2. With Select, double-click the free complex's ring bond near
   (1818,1100) and drag that point to the right-hand arc near
   (1464,1238) in the captured window.
3. Release. The app reports "Added to the cycle as Intermediate 2".
   Deselect on empty canvas to see the finished placement.
4. Undo once to return the complex to its starting position. Redo once
   to put it back in the cycle. Save and compare the two .rsk files.

The saved result keeps all 154 bonds, all 136 atoms, the existing
intermediate order and the two named step roles. Only the 46 atoms
of the inserted complex moved. The newly created step is unnamed.
The in-flight arc highlight and tag are exercised in
dragging_onto_an_arc_lights_it_and_tags_below_the_piece. The native
screen captures show the starting drawing and the states after release.
