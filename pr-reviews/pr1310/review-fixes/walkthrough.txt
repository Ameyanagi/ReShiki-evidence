1. Open Icons, search for "percentage 40", then place the matching icon.
   The catalogue and placed icon show the same filled wedge and outer ring.
2. Close Icons and open Transform in Properties. Both dimensions read
   16.44 pt. The proportions toggle is checked and disabled for the icon.
3. Enter width 24 pt and Apply. Both dimensions now read 24.00 pt.
   One Undo restores 16.44 pt in both fields. Redo restores 24.00 pt.
4. Save. The first path in percentage-40.rsk retains filled=true and
   stroked=false. The separate outer ring retains its stroke.
