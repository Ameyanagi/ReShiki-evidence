## Summary

The molecule's name can now be the first line of its property caption, in bold, with Mol. Wt., Exact Mass or the other properties below it. **Show properties ›** has a **Name** row at the top, and **Show chemical name** shows the same row. The name is the automatic (IUPAC) name, or a custom name such as `Compound 3a` typed in the new **Name** field of **Molecular properties** or by double-clicking the name on the canvas.

Draft: implementation and tests are in; evidence and full checks follow.
